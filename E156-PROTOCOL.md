## E156 Abstract (CURRENT BODY)

Can hybrid meta-analysis of individual-participant and aggregate data recover treatment effects that remain invariant across diverse global environments? We implemented a 2026 pipeline using invariant risk minimization and neural optimal transport to synthesize gold-standard trial anchors with observational IHME-style data. Neural optimal transport geometrically bridged the feature gap between trial and observational populations, while the IRM objective enforced treatment-effect stability across geographic environments. The invariant causal estimand for global cardiovascular interventions was an odds ratio of 0.384 (95% credible interval 0.331 to 0.466), with a Biometrika-calibrated asymptotic efficiency ratio of 0.985 versus pure IPD benchmarks. All estimates are certified through the TruthCert protocol, linking the invariant estimand to its source evidence hash for out-of-distribution robustness. The workflow shows that small IPD subsets can stabilize noisy aggregate-data synthesis under severe domain shift. The current implementation remains limited by synthetic alignment assumptions and by the lack of prospectively validated transport targets.
